# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Single self-contained `index.html` (Three.js from CDN) plus a local `assets/` folder. Chosen for an easily shareable client pitch.

## Users

Primary: café owners and retail outlets buying cookies wholesale, and food brands looking for a contract (white-label) cookie manufacturer. Secondary: consumers buying café cookies and Mini Bites buckets online.

## Product Purpose

Divine Foods Aust. is a cookie manufacturer in South Windsor NSW (Est. 1997). The site must turn wholesale and contract-manufacturing enquiries into conversations, while keeping the online shop reachable. This build is a redesign pitch by Cre8tive Sync to win the web development engagement.

## Positioning

An Australian maker since 1997 that bakes café-format individually wrapped cookies, bulk Mini Bites buckets, and discreetly makes other brands' cookies ("We can make your cookies for you and nobody will know").

## Brand Commitments

- Wordmark: black heavy condensed caps "DIVINE FOODS AUST" with underline bar.
- Taglines: "Seductively Good", "Give in to a little bit of sugar".

## Product Principles

1. B2B enquiries first, shop always one click away.
2. Indulgence is the brand: sensual, confident, a little cheeky.
